01The problem

In a crisis, the picture arrives late and in pieces.

Damage reports, fire alerts, power cuts and road closures live in different places. RescueForce brings scattered information together so responders can understand the impact and see where support is needed.

Slow to see

Ground reports can take days, and some areas are too risky to reach at all.

Scattered data

Damage, fires, night lights, population and roads sit in separate tools and files.

Hard to trust

A rumour and a satellite observation often look the same on a map.

02How it works

From satellite data to better decisions.

Step 1

Satellites detect

Radar, night-light and thermal satellites spot possible changes on the ground, day or night, through cloud.

Step 2

RescueForce analyses

Its AI agents connect the evidence, rank the impact on people and services, and suggest next checks and routes.

Step 3

Responders decide

Officers review the findings, check them on the ground and make the call. Suggestions never act on their own.

04AI agents

AI that suggests. People decide.

Four small agents read the satellite evidence and turn it into plain answers. They explain their reasons, show what is still unknown, and never act on their own.

Ranking agent

Orders incidents by people affected, damage, fires, power loss and how fresh the data is.

“Khiam east first: most people nearby and two satellites agree.”

Insights agent

Writes 3–5 short findings for each incident, each with its evidence label and source date.

“Radar change runs along the eastern ridge road.” Observed

Decision assistant

Suggests next checks, route options and nearby shelter sites. Every idea is marked unverified.

“Ask a field team to check the ridge road before routing vehicles.”

Rebuild budget

Gives an indicative cost range for each area, with the assumptions listed.

“4.2–9.8 million USD · indicative, not a quote.”
05Evidence you can trust

Know what is observed, reported and still uncertain.

Observed

Seen by a sensor

Detected through available satellite observations.

Reported

Told by a source

Shared by a field team, a municipality or the media.

Corroborated

Backed twice

Supported by more than one independent source.

Unknown

Not enough yet

Too little evidence to say. RescueForce shows the gap instead of guessing.

Candidates, not facts.Every damage area, route and shelter is a candidate until someone checks it on the ground. Numbers are shown as ranges, and nothing is ever labelled safe.
